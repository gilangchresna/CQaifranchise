## 🎤 Key Scripts

### Opening Statement
> "Good morning. Today I'll show you how we're transforming franchise operations with AI.
> 
> **The Problem:** Franchise operators manage multiple outlets across countries, but they're flying blind.
> 
> **Our Solution:** A real-time AI platform that monitors every outlet, detects anomalies instantly, and takes action automatically.
> 
> **The Result:** Faster response, less revenue loss, happier franchisees."

### Dashboard Demo
> "Here's the dashboard — **real data from 34 outlets across 6 countries**.
> 
> Notice the **country filter** — I can switch from Singapore to Indonesia, and all data updates instantly. Currency is automatically converted.
> 
> **KPI cards** show revenue, active outlets, low stock alerts, and system health — all in real-time."

### AI Anomaly Detection
> "This is where the magic happens. Our AI analyzes **86,726 transactions** and detects anomalies in real-time.
> 
> **Watch this alert** — AI detected a sales drop of 40% at outlet SG-003. It automatically created a case, assigned priority, and notified the regional manager.
> 
> **Response time: minutes, not days.**"

### Athena AI Chat
> "Meet **Athena** — your AI assistant. Ask her anything about your franchise.
> 
> *Demo: 'What's the total revenue for Singapore this week?'*
> 
> Athena responds instantly with **S$ 4.7M** — currency-aware, context-aware, powered by Claude AI.
> 
> She can analyze trends, predict stockouts, and recommend actions."

### Bridge Financing
> "Franchisees need working capital. Our **Bridge Financing** module lets them apply for loans directly.
> 
> **PDPA Compliant** — before submitting, franchisees must consent to data sharing with lenders. We have region-specific policies for Singapore, Indonesia, and Malaysia.
> 
> **Future:** Document Vault will allow secure upload of KYC and financial documents."

### Closing Statement
> "This is **production-ready**. We have:
> - 34 outlets live
> - 8 AI agents running 24/7
> - Multi-currency, multi-country
> - PDPA compliant
> - Real-time anomaly detection
> 
> **Next step:** Pilot program with your franchisees. We can onboard 10 outlets in 2 weeks."

## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Demo data looks fake | Use real data, mention "86K transactions" |
| AI responds slowly | Pre-test responses, have fallback answers |
| Login fails | Test login before demo, have backup account |
| Edge function error | Test all endpoints before demo |
| Currency shows wrong | Verify currency display, mention multi-currency |
