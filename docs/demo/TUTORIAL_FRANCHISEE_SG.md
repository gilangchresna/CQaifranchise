## 📋 Overview

As a **Franchisee Owner**, you have access to:
- View your own outlets in Singapore
- Monitor sales and inventory for your outlets
- Apply for Bridge Financing
- Chat with Athena AI

**Note:** You CANNOT see other franchisees' data or access admin features.

## 🚨 Step 3: Alerts for Your Outlets

### Accessing Alerts:
1. Click **Active Escalations** in sidebar
2. See only alerts for YOUR outlets
3. Each alert shows: Outlet, Type, Severity

### Alert Types:
| Type | What It Means |
|------|---------------|
| SALES_ANOMALY | Unusual sales pattern detected |
| STOCKOUT_RISK | Low inventory risk |
| LOW_STOCK | Item running low |

### Your Action:
1. Click on alert to view details
2. Review AI recommendation
3. Acknowledge or create case

## 🤖 Step 4: Athena AI Chat

### Accessing Athena:
1. Click **blue chat bubble** (bottom-right)

### Try These Questions:
| Question | Expected Response |
|----------|-------------------|
| "What's my revenue this week?" | S$ amount for your outlets |
| "Which products are low stock?" | Your outlet's low stock items |

### Athena Features:
- ✅ **Scope:** Only sees YOUR outlets
- ✅ **Currency:** Always S$ (SGD)

## 🏦 Step 5: Bridge Financing

### Accessing Bridge Financing:
1. Click **Bridge Financing** in sidebar
2. See your financing applications

### Applying for a Loan:
1. Click **Apply for Loan**
2. **Consent Dialog** appears:

#### PDPA Consent Flow:
| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Read Privacy Notice (Singapore PDPA 2012) |
| 2 | Check consent checkbox |
| 3 | Click **I Consent** |
| 4 | Fill loan application form |
| 5 | Submit application |

#### Consent Dialog Details:
- **Policy:** PDPA Privacy Notice — Singapore
- **Legal Basis:** Personal Data Protection Act 2012
- **Data Shared:** Business financials, transaction history
- **Purpose:** Loan assessment by lending partner

### Loan Application Form:
| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Amount | Loan amount in S$ |
| Purpose | Working capital, equipment, renovation |
| Term | Repayment period |
| Outlet | Which outlet needs financing |
