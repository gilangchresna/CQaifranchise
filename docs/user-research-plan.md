# User Research Plan

## CyberQuote MVP — Sprint 0

> Understanding user needs before building the product.

---

## 1. RESEARCH OBJECTIVES

### Primary Goals

1. **Validate pain points** — Confirm that stockouts and inventory issues are the #1 operational pain point
2. **Understand current workflows** — Map existing processes for inventory management and ordering
3. **Identify requirements** — Gather specific features and integrations needed
4. **Establish success metrics** — Define what "success" looks like for each user type

### Secondary Goals

- Uncover hidden opportunities or unmet needs
- Identify potential adoption barriers
- Understand decision-making authority and approval processes

---

## 2. TARGET AUDIENCE

| Segment | Role | Interview Count | Goal |
|---------|------|-----------------|------|
| **A** | Franchise Owners | 5 | Pain points, current solutions, ROI expectations |
| **B** | Regional/Area Managers | 3 | Monitoring needs, reporting, oversight workflows |
| **C** | HQ Administrators | 2 | Reporting needs, data requirements, integration |

### Participant Criteria

**Must have:**
- Active involvement in inventory management
- Decision authority for ordering/supply
- Minimum 1 year in current role

**Nice to have:**
- Experience with previous tech solutions
- Multiple outlet responsibility
- Involvement in franchise operations

---

## 3. INTERVIEW GUIDE

### Interview Format

- **Duration**: 45–60 minutes
- **Method**: Video call (Zoom/Google Meet)
- **Participants**: 1 interviewer, 1 note-taker
- **Recording**: With permission, for transcription

---

### 3.1 FRANCHISE OWNERS (Segment A)

#### Opening (5 min)
- Thank participant
- Explain purpose: "Help us build the right tool for your business"
- Confirm permission to take notes/record

#### Background (10 min)

**Q1:** "Tell me about your outlet(s). How many do you manage, and what are your hours like?"

**Q2:** "Walk me through a typical day. What are the first things you check when you arrive?"

**Q3:** "What does inventory management look like for you right now?"

#### Pain Points (15 min)

**Q4:** "What's your biggest challenge when it comes to stock and inventory?"

> Probe: Is it about knowing what's running low? Timing of orders? Supplier issues?

**Q5:** "Tell me about a time when you ran out of something important. What happened?"

> Probe: How did you find out? What was the impact? Could it have been prevented?

**Q6:** "What does the ordering process look like now? Walk me through it step by step."

> Probe: How often? Who decides? What factors into the decision?

**Q7:** "When you think about a 'perfect' inventory system, what does it do for you?"

#### Current Solutions (10 min)

**Q8:** "What tools or methods do you use today to track inventory?"

> Probe: Spreadsheets? POS reports? Intuition? Pen and paper?

**Q9:** "Have you tried any technology solutions before? What happened?"

> Probe: What worked? What didn't? Why did you stop?

**Q10:** "What's your comfort level with smartphones and WhatsApp for business tasks?"

#### Impact & Expectations (10 min)

**Q11:** "If we could solve one inventory problem for you, what would it be?"

**Q12:** "What would success look like? How would you measure if this tool is working?"

**Q13:** "What's the maximum you'd be willing to pay for a solution that fixes this problem?"

> Probe: One-time vs. recurring? Per outlet?

#### Wrap-up (5 min)

**Q14:** "Is there anything else about inventory management that I should know?"

**Q15:** "Do you have colleagues who might be interested in being part of this pilot?"

---

### 3.2 REGIONAL MANAGERS (Segment B)

#### Background (5 min)

**Q1:** "How many outlets do you oversee, and what's your typical day like?"

**Q2:** "How do you currently keep track of what's happening across your outlets?"

#### Oversight Challenges (15 min)

**Q3:** "What's your biggest challenge when it comes to overseeing inventory across multiple outlets?"

**Q4:** "How do you know when an outlet is running low on critical items?"

**Q5:** "Tell me about a time when poor inventory management at one outlet affected the region."

**Q6:** "What visibility do you wish you had that you don't currently have?"

#### Reporting & Analysis (10 min)

**Q7:** "What reports or data do you need to make ordering decisions?"

**Q8:** "How often do you review inventory data? What format do you prefer?"

**Q9:** "What would an ideal dashboard look like for you?"

#### Alert Preferences (10 min)

**Q10:** "How do you currently hear about stockouts or urgent issues?"

**Q11:** "What types of alerts would be most valuable to you?"

> Probe: Outlets needing attention? Trends? Exceptions?

**Q12:** "What's your preferred way to receive urgent notifications?"

#### Success & Adoption (10 min)

**Q13:** "What would make you confident this system is working?"

**Q14:** "What would make your outlets actually use this tool?"

**Q15:** "What concerns do you have about this type of system?"

#### Wrap-up (5 min)

**Q16:** "Anything else I should know about managing inventory across outlets?"

---

### 3.3 HQ ADMINISTRATORS (Segment C)

#### Background (5 min)

**Q1:** "What's your role in the organization and what data are you responsible for?"

**Q2:** "Walk me through how inventory decisions are made at the corporate level."

#### Reporting & Analytics (15 min)

**Q3:** "What reporting do you need from franchise operations?"

**Q4:** "What KPIs or metrics are most important to track?"

**Q5:** "How do you currently consolidate data from multiple outlets?"

**Q6:** "What would an ideal analytics view look like?"

#### Integration Requirements (10 min)

**Q7:** "What systems does your organization currently use for POS, inventory, or ordering?"

**Q8:** "Are there any mandatory integrations or compliance requirements?"

**Q9:** "How is data security and privacy handled in your organization?"

#### Decision-Making (10 min)

**Q10:** "What would make you recommend this solution to franchise owners?"

**Q11:** "What ROI or cost-benefit analysis would be required?"

**Q12:** "Who else in HQ would need to approve a new tool like this?"

#### Wrap-up (5 min)

**Q13:** "What's the biggest barrier to adopting new technology in your franchise?"

**Q14:** "Anything else about operations reporting I should know?"

## 5. ANALYSIS FRAMEWORK

### Affinity Mapping

After each interview, record insights as cards:

```
[INSIGHT] Franchise owners rely on "gut feel" more than data
[SOURCE] Q4 - 3 of 5 franchise owners
[IMPLICATION] System must feel easy, not add to cognitive load
```

Group cards into themes:
- Pain points
- Current workflows
- Feature requests
- Adoption barriers
- Pricing sensitivity

### Persona Development

Create 2–3 user personas based on interview findings:

| Persona | Characteristics | Primary Need |
|---------|-----------------|--------------|
| The Hands-On Owner | Manages 1-3 outlets, does ordering personally | Simple, fast alerts |
| The Delegating Manager | Oversees 5-15 outlets, trains staff | Visibility + control |
| The Data-Driven HQ | Sets policy, reviews reports | Aggregate insights |
